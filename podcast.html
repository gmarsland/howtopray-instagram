The podcast

One question at a time, for the person asking it.

Each episode is for someone in particular: the one who is sick, a frightened parent, a tired carer, someone who has never prayed for anyone, someone still waiting, someone not sure God still heals, someone who leads others, someone getting to know Jesus. It answers one question that person is already asking, from the book and the Bible, and ends with a prayer you can pray aloud. Start with the one that is yours.

Each episode opens and closes with Graham. The two voices in between are made with artificial intelligence. They are not Graham, and they have no stories of their own. Everything they say comes from the book and from the Bible. Graham listens to every episode before it is put here.

When you are the one who is sick

  • Episode 1 · 11 minutesDoes God Want Me Well?At Marah the water was too bitter to drink, and there God told His people one of His names: “I am the LORD who heals you.” For anyone who is sick and wonders whether God wants them well.
  • Episode 9 · 16 minutesWhat Do I Hold On To When the Symptoms Shout?A Roman centurion told Jesus, “just say the word.” For anyone whose symptoms seem louder than God's promise.

When your child is sick

  • Episode 2 · 13 minutesHow Do I Pray for My Child When I Am Frightened?A father brought his son to Jesus and cried out, “I do believe; help my unbelief!” For every parent who is frightened for their child.
  • Episode 10 · 14 minutesDoes God Love My Child More Than I Do?If you know how to give good gifts to your children, how much more will your Father in heaven? For every parent of a sick child.

When you are caring for someone

  • Episode 3 · 19 minutesWhat Do I Say When I Pray for Someone I Love?A simple, kind way to pray for someone you love, one step at a time, and a prayer Graham prayed for his friend in a car.
  • Episode 11 · 11 minutesHow Do I Love Them Well While They Are Sick?Jesus took a blind man by the hand and led him out of the village. For anyone caring for someone they love.

When you have never prayed for anyone

  • Episode 4 · 20 minutesIs It Really My Place to Pray for the Sick?Ananias was an ordinary follower of Jesus when God sent him to lay his hands on Saul. For anyone who thinks praying for the sick is only for pastors.
  • Episode 12 · 20 minutesWhat If I Pray and Look Foolish?On Mount Carmel, Elijah prayed in front of everyone. For anyone afraid of looking foolish if they pray for someone.

When you are still waiting

  • Episode 5 · 13 minutesIs It My Fault I Am Still Sick?For anyone who has prayed and is still sick. Why a person who is still sick is never to blame, and why it is right to keep asking.

When you are not sure God still heals

  • Episode 6 · 15 minutesDoes God Still Heal Today?An honest account of a young woman in Perth, as the book tells it, and why every story like it points to Jesus.

When you lead others

  • Episode 7 · 20 minutesHow Do I Lead My Church to Pray for the Sick?Jesus sent seventy-two of His followers out two by two. A simple, kind way for a church or a small group to begin praying for the sick.

When you are getting to know Jesus

  • Episode 8 · 22 minutesWhat Is Jesus Like?Blind Bartimaeus kept calling out when the crowd told him to be quiet, and Jesus stopped. For anyone still finding out who Jesus is.
